Bink logo lab

Two sets of directions for the Bink logo: clean and simple first, then loud and playful. Every one uses the bright green ball, so any of them could slot into the site.

Clean & simple

Quiet shapes, lots of space, a single touch of green.

Baseline C7

The word rests on a white court line, with the ball waiting at the end of it. Simple and sporty.

Two overlapping circles, two players meeting. A quiet nod to doubles and to community.
