FAQ

Questions? We've got you.

Is Bink free?

Yes. Matching, chatting and the Bink Open are free for beta members.

What kind of events?

Drop-in nights, skills clinics, social mixers, King of the Hill and tournaments, all sorted by level.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Most people do. Events are built for meeting new players.

New, or unsure of my level?

Pick the level that feels closest. You can change it any time.

Can I find a coach?

Yes. Browse coaches near you, read reviews, and book lessons or a clinic.

Is it safe to meet people?

You choose who you play with and chat first. You can report or block anyone.
