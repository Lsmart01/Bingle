Champion of the Court

What the agreed design looks like on a phone: Fernwood's board, logging and confirming a game, taking the paddle, a player's profile, doubles, and hiding your courts. These are mockups from the spec, not the built app.

Sample players at Fernwood:Alex Chen (Queen)Sam PatelJordan Lee (you, non-binary → Champion)Ben OkaforPriya ShahTheo Park

Fernwood's board

One board per court, last 30 days, most wins first, then point difference. The champion's card sits on top with the golden paddle. Your personal card shows below it only for you.

Title comes from gender:woman → Queenman → Kinganyone else or blank → Champion

Logging and confirming

Logging a win shows where you'd land straight away. The result counts once the other side confirms, or automatically after 24 hours unless they dispute it.

Taking the paddle

The first time the new champion opens the app, once per court. No Share button for now.

Tapping a player

Every name and photo on a board opens that player's profile, with their record at the court you were looking at.

Doubles, including mixed

Mixed games count on the doubles board. Both partners hold the title, and the pair's titles combine.

Hiding where you play

Players who hide their courts are left off boards entirely. Their games still count for their opponents, and they can still see their own stats. Here Priya has turned the setting on.
