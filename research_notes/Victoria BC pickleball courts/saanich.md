# Pickleball courts in the District of Saanich, BC (current to November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Main sources: saanich.ca pages (live in Nov 2026), Saanich's Sept 2024 open-house boards, the Saanich news release of Sept 1, 2026, Victoria News of Sept 8, 2026, the Victoria Regional Pickleball Association (VRPA) "Where to Play" page (undated, but it mentions the Oct 1 Tolmie closure, so it is likely from 2026), and the Vikes Rec (UVic) pickleball page (fees valid as of Aug 18, 2026).

## Q1: Which Saanich parks have outdoor pickleball courts or lines as of 2026?

### Takeaway
As of Nov 2026, Saanich's official list has **6 outdoor locations with 18 pickleball courts: 10 shared (lines on tennis courts) and 8 dedicated**. They are Copley West, Hampton, McMinn, Rosedale, Prospect Lake and the new Fowler Park hub. **Tolmie Park's 2 dedicated courts closed permanently on Oct 1, 2026.** Majestic Park's single shared court is no longer on the official list, which suggests its pickleball lines have been removed (not confirmed).

### Venue-by-venue inventory (outdoor)

**General rules for all Saanich outdoor courts:** pickleball hours are 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily unless otherwise noted. Shared courts are first-come, first-served. Tennis hours are 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. Play is free, except the paid reservations at Fowler. — [Saanich Outdoor Tennis and Pickleball Courts](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation-community/parks/parks-trails-amenities/amenities/tennis-courts.html) (live page, weekly schedule shown for Nov 23–29, 2026)

1. **Fowler Park (Cordova Bay)**, next to the Lochside Regional Trail, near Lochside Park
   - **Courts:** 6 dedicated outdoor pickleball courts, plus 1 dedicated tennis court. — [Saanich release, Sept 1, 2026](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/news-events/news-archives/2026-news/saanich-to-pilot-reservation-system-for-new-fowler-park-pickleball-courts.html)
   - **Opening:** opened for public play Sept 15, 2026. — [Saanich release](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/news-events/news-archives/2026-news/saanich-to-pilot-reservation-system-for-new-fowler-park-pickleball-courts.html)
   - **Booking pilot (Sept 15 to Oct 31, 2026):** 4 courts reservable at $10.50/hr (tax included) through Saanich RecOnline (saanich.ca/register), in 1- or 2-hour slots, up to 7 days ahead. Bookings open at 7:30 a.m. — [Saanich courts page](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation-community/parks/parks-trails-amenities/amenities/tennis-courts.html)
   - **Unbooked times and walk-up courts:** 2 courts are always first-come, first-served. Any court with no reservation reverts to first-come, first-served. The tennis court is first-come, first-served only. — [Saanich release](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/news-events/news-archives/2026-news/saanich-to-pilot-reservation-system-for-new-fowler-park-pickleball-courts.html)
   - **After the pilot:** the system will be assessed and may change before reservations return in spring 2027. Victoria News quotes the fee as $10/hr; the official figure is $10.50 with tax. — [Victoria News, Sept 8, 2026](https://vicnews.com/2026/09/08/here-to-stay-saanich-answers-pickleball-demand-with-fowler-park-soft-launch/)
   - **Cost and amenities:** $2.4M project. Construction began Nov 2025. Includes 38 parking stalls, a raised viewing area with seating, seasonal shade umbrellas and a water fountain. Courts sit below road level, with berms and trees, and an acoustic engineer was consulted. It was a "soft launch"; **full opening is targeted for late April 2027** because landscaping is unfinished. — [Victoria News](https://vicnews.com/2026/09/08/here-to-stay-saanich-answers-pickleball-demand-with-fowler-park-soft-launch/)

   - **VRPA listing:** "6 dedicated courts," drop-in or reserve through Saanich's booking calendar. — [VRPA](https://secure.pickleballcanada.org/club/victoria-regional-pickleball-association/1033/where-to-play/7652/)

### Inferences
- **Count reconciliation:** the official count works out as Copley 2 + Hampton 2 + Rosedale 2 + Prospect Lake 2 + McMinn 2 = 10 shared, and McMinn 2 + Fowler 6 = 8 dedicated. That means Majestic (1 shared) and Tolmie (2 dedicated) are excluded. Majestic's lines were therefore probably removed after Fowler opened.
- **Lights and nets:** no source mentions lights at any Saanich outdoor pickleball court. The 9 a.m.–8 p.m. hours suggest courts are unlit, or that lights are irrelevant to pickleball.
  - Shared courts most likely use the tennis net (players lower it or play over it). Players may bring portable nets; this is unverified.
  - Dedicated courts (McMinn, Fowler) most likely have permanent pickleball nets; also unverified.
- **For an app:** in practice, the useful Saanich outdoor spots are Fowler (6 dedicated, bookable) and McMinn (4). The shared courts get heavy use (Rosedale) but are contested with tennis players.

## Q2: Which Saanich rec centres run indoor pickleball and when?

### Takeaway
Saanich runs indoor pickleball at three municipal rec centres:
- **Saanich Commonwealth Place (SCP):** reserved drop-in, Mon–Fri.
- **G.R. Pearkes:** court reservations, about 8 courts.
- **Cedar Hill:** court rentals, 2 courts.

Saanich Silver Threads (seniors' centre) runs drop-in sessions. UVic's CARSA (in Saanich) has the largest program, with a pass or membership required. No current pickleball was found at Gordon Head Rec Centre or Camosun.

## Q3: New courts opened or planned (2024–2026), and courts restricted or closed over noise

### Takeaway
The big change is **Fowler Park: 6 dedicated courts plus 1 tennis court opened Sept 15, 2026**, with a paid booking pilot. Alongside it, **Tolmie Park closed Oct 1, 2026** after years of noise complaints, and Majestic's shared lines were slated for removal. **Copley West courts are locked outside 9 a.m.–8 p.m.** because of off-hours play. Planned shared-to-dedicated conversions at Copley West, Hampton and Rosedale are **on hold**. McMinn's conversion to 4 dedicated courts with sound mitigation was tentatively set for 2026, and its status is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Fowler timeline:**
  - A 2008 concept plan had 1 tennis court. A 2021 survey showed demand, and the active-hub concept was approved in 2023.
  - Construction began Nov 2025, with a soft launch Sept 15, 2026 and full opening targeted for late April 2027.
  - Crews had to replace blue clay under the site.
  - Parks senior manager Eva Riccius: pickleball is "here to stay."
  — [Victoria News](https://vicnews.com/2026/09/08/here-to-stay-saanich-answers-pickleball-demand-with-fowler-park-soft-launch/)
- **Fowler delay:** the 2024 boards estimated completion in "late 2025," so it was about 9 months late. — [Saanich open-house boards](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Parks~Recreation~and~Community~Services/Documents/Pickleball-Tennis-OpenHouse-FINAL.pdf)
- **Tolmie closed Oct 1, 2026:** the noise history is under Q1 item 7. — [Saanich courts page](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation-community/parks/parks-trails-amenities/amenities/tennis-courts.html); [Saanich open-house boards](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Parks~Recreation~and~Community~Services/Documents/Pickleball-Tennis-OpenHouse-FINAL.pdf)
- **Copley West lock-up:** courts locked outside 9 a.m.–8 p.m. "until further notice" because of regularly reported pickleball outside permitted hours. — [Saanich courts page](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation-community/parks/parks-trails-amenities/amenities/tennis-courts.html)
- **Conversions ON HOLD:** Copley West (2026 trial), Hampton and Rosedale (2027–28). — [Saanich plan page](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation-community/parks/projects-in-saanich-parks/pickleball-and-tennis-in-outdoor-parks.html)
- **McMinn:** convert to 4 dedicated pickleball courts plus 1 tennis court, add sound mitigation and a community-funded shelter, "tentatively 2026," subject to budget. — [Saanich plan page](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation-community/parks/projects-in-saanich-parks/pickleball-and-tennis-in-outdoor-parks.html)
- **Booking model:** Saanich plans a mixed system of paid individual and organization bookings plus free public drop-in, with Paddle-In/Paddle-Out at dedicated courts. — [Saanich open-house boards](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Parks~Recreation~and~Community~Services/Documents/Pickleball-Tennis-OpenHouse-FINAL.pdf)
- **North Saanich is out of scope:** the widely reported 2024 Wain Park closure was in North Saanich, a separate municipality. — [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/10468571/pickleball-north-saanich-closure)

### Inferences
- **Net change in 2026:** dedicated outdoor courts went from 4 (Tolmie 2 + McMinn 2) to 8 (McMinn 2 + Fowler 6). Shared courts fell from 11 to 10 (Majestic dropped).
