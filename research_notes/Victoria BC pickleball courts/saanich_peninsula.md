# Saanich Peninsula (Sidney, North Saanich, Central Saanich) Pickleball Court Inventory, as of Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-24. The peninsula is small. At last check it had about 4 active public venue clusters plus 1 closed site. Municipal and Panorama Recreation pages were fetched directly. Court directories (Pickleheads, Bounce, Play Pickleball) are often out of date or conflict with each other, as noted below. northsaanich.ca and pickleheads.com returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches, so their content comes only from search snippets.

## Which parks and facilities have pickleball? (location-by-location inventory)

### Takeaway
Central Saanich's Centennial Park (Saanichton) is now the peninsula's main hub. It has 4 covered, lit courts in the Multi-Sport Box, run by Panorama Recreation since Sept 2025, plus the Court #1 racquet courts across the road. Court #1 was relined in Aug–Sept 2026 into 4 dedicated courts (1A–1D). Sidney has only small shared/multi-use courts at Iroquois Park. North Saanich's only dedicated outdoor courts, Wain Road Park at Birch Rd, were permanently closed in May 2024 over noise. Indoor and covered drop-in play runs through Panorama Recreation at Panorama Rec Centre, Greenglade and North Saanich Middle School.

### Cited Findings

**1. Centennial Park Multi-Sport Box ("Sport Box"), Centennial Park, 1859 Hovey Rd, Saanichton, Central Saanich** (approx. lat/long 48.59, -123.42, an estimate not taken from a source)
- Opened for community use Sept 2, 2025. It replaces the aging lacrosse box. It has a durable roof (covered, open-air), LED lighting for evening and night use, and is lined for pickleball, lacrosse and ball hockey — [Central Saanich, July 29, 2025](https://www.centralsaanich.ca/node/3617)
- Operated by the Capital Regional District (CRD) through the Peninsula Recreation Commission (Panorama Recreation) as a regional amenity. Central Saanich provided the land and a $2M federal grant. Drop-in is available when the box isn't booked for programs or events. Schedules and bookings go through Panorama — [Central Saanich, July 29, 2025](https://www.centralsaanich.ca/node/3617)
- 4 pickleball courts with painted lines; nets provided. Booked through Panorama Recreation. Panorama now also manages the Fieldhouse and picnic shelters — [Central Saanich Park Bookings, undated, fetched Nov 2026](https://www.centralsaanich.ca/node/220)
- "4 covered open air courts," listed as opening Sept 2025, with a booking request form — [Victoria Regional Pickleball Association (VRPA) where-to-play](https://secure.pickleballcanada.org/club/victoria-regional-pickleball-association/1033/where-to-play/7652/)
- Panorama runs "Open Court" times at the Sport Box. Pickleball nets are available during Open Court, and Program Monitors lend paddles and eye protection. Reservations are recommended; drop-in is welcome when space permits — [Panorama Recreation, Drop-in Sports](https://www.panoramarecreation.ca/drop-activities/sports)
- Pickleheads lists 4 courts, hours 7am–10pm, and says reservations have priority. This is a snippet only, since the page returned 403 — [Pickleheads](https://www.pickleheads.com/courts/ca/british-columbia/saanichton/centennial-park-multi-sport-box)
- Surface type is not stated in any source.

**2. Centennial Park Racquet Courts (Court #1, courts 1A–1D), Centennial Park, 1859 Hovey Rd, Saanichton** (across the road from the Sport Box)
- June 25, 2026: Council approved relining Court #1, then lined for both tennis and pickleball, into 4 dedicated pickleball courts. Work was scheduled for August into September 2026. The District said it "currently offers six pickleball courts," meaning 4 in the Sport Box plus 2 shared-use courts at the Racquet Courts, and that the upgrades would raise overall capacity to 8 — [Central Saanich, June 25, 2026](https://www.centralsaanich.ca/node/3989)
- VRPA lists Centennial Park as "2 lined tennis courts," reservable through Central Saanich, and "Closed for court conversion for the month of August, 2026" — [VRPA](https://secure.pickleballcanada.org/club/victoria-regional-pickleball-association/1033/where-to-play/7652/)
- Current bookings page: Courts 1A and 1B are first-come, first-served unless reserved by permit, and reservable online through the [Central Saanich Online Reservations](https://app.univerusrec.com/centralsaanichpub/) system. Courts 1C and 1D are first-come, first-served only. Open daily 8am–9pm. Fee: adults $6.75/hr, free for youth and non-profit/charitable groups. The fee appears under the tennis and pickleball section, but the page doesn't say clearly whether it applies to pickleball bookings. Rules include a 30-minute or one-set limit when others are waiting, doubles when busy, and "courts are for pickleball only" — [Central Saanich Park Bookings, fetched Nov 2026](https://www.centralsaanich.ca/node/220)
- History: an earlier pilot put 2 pickleball courts in the old lacrosse box, first-come first-served — [Central Saanich Pilot Project, undated](https://www.centralsaanich.ca/node/2116). Council later kept the status quo of one pickleball court on Court #1 — [Peninsula News Review (syndicated), undated](https://bpbpd.wpengine.com/news/central-saanich-puts-pickleball-off-to-parks-master-plan-discussion). In 2017, tennis players objected to pickleball lines on the Centennial tennis courts — [VicNews, Aug 28, 2017](https://www.vicnews.com/community/north-saanichs-new-pickleball-courts-welcomed-by-players/)
- Lights and nets for Court #1 are not stated in any source.

**3. Iroquois Park, 2295 Ocean Ave, Sidney** (next to Tulista Park, near the waterfront; approx. lat/long 48.645, -123.398, an estimate)
- Two tennis courts, one with pickleball lines. Tennis and pickleball share it, with a 45-minute limit when others are waiting. A separate multi-sport court (basketball hoops, street hockey nets) also has painted pickleball lines. A portable pickleball net is kept in a lock-up tube on the fence, combo "PLAY." Lights are not mentioned. Free and first-come — [Town of Sidney, Iroquois Park page, fetched Nov 2026](https://www.sidney.ca/parks/iroquois-park/)
- VRPA lists "2 dedicated courts," likely counting the lined tennis court and the multi-sport court. Pickleheads and Bounce list 1 court and note a taller tennis net — [VRPA](https://secure.pickleballcanada.org/club/victoria-regional-pickleball-association/1033/where-to-play/7652/); [Pickleheads](https://www.pickleheads.com/courts/ca/british-columbia/sidney/iroquois-park). The court count conflicts between sources. By the Town's own description, these are shared or multi-use courts, not truly dedicated ones.

**7. Wain Road Park (Wain Rd / Birch Rd), North Saanich. CLOSED.**
- 4 dedicated outdoor courts officially opened Aug 11, 2017, at Birch Road. They saw about 100 players a day. North Saanich was then the only peninsula council to build courts — [VicNews, Aug 28, 2017](https://www.vicnews.com/community/north-saanichs-new-pickleball-courts-welcomed-by-players/)
- On Apr 29, 2024, Council directed permanent closure of the Wain Road courts effective 7:30am May 7, 2024, rather than paying for sound mitigation — [North Saanich, Wain Road Park Pickleball Courts](https://northsaanich.ca/post/wain-road-park-pickleball-courts); [Capital Daily](https://www.capitaldaily.ca/news/turn-out-the-lights-the-pickleball-is-over-on-these-north-saanich-courts); [CHEK](https://www.cheknews.ca/this-was-not-a-snap-decision-north-saanich-votes-to-close-wain-park-pickleball-courts-1202368/)
- The council balked at $50K–$70K sound-mitigation fencing. In July 2024, an offer of sound-control help got a cool reception — [Times Colonist via PressReader, July 10, 2024](https://pressreader.com/canada/times-colonist/20240710/281492166534769)
- In Feb 2025, supporters dropped their request for a judicial review of the closure — [Capital Daily](https://www.capitaldaily.ca/news/judicial-review-request-rescinded-north-saanich-pickleball-saga-plays-on)
- The District's pickleball page still shows the courts as closed until further notice. This comes from a search snippet; the page itself returns 403 — [northsaanich.ca/pickleball](https://northsaanich.ca/pickleball)

### Inferences
- The Central Saanich bookings page now lists four courts, 1A–1D, with 1C and 1D first-come only. That strongly suggests the Court #1 conversion to 4 dedicated courts finished around Sept 2026. The District's "8 courts" total means 4 in the Sport Box plus 4 on Court #1, which implies the previous 2 shared courts were absorbed into the 4 new ones rather than added to them.
- Realistic public outdoor court count, Nov 2026: Central Saanich about 8 (4 covered and lit, 4 open-air), Sidney about 1–2 shared/multi-use, North Saanich 0 outdoor (Wain closed). Indoor/covered drop-in comes through Panorama: Greenglade (2), Panorama Rec Jumpstart court, and NSMS.
- Panorama Recreation, run by the CRD for all three peninsula municipalities, is the single booking and drop-in operator for every indoor or covered venue.

## Indoor drop-in schedules (Panorama Recreation)

### Takeaway
Panorama Recreation runs pickleball as reservable drop-in or "Open Court" sessions at the Panorama Rec Centre Jumpstart court, the Centennial Park Sport Box, Greenglade Community Centre and North Saanich Middle School. Bookings open weekly, on Fridays, for the following week. I couldn't retrieve a day-by-day timetable for fall/winter 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Reservations are recommended, with drop-in welcome when space permits. Sessions open for reservation each Friday for the upcoming calendar week: at 7:00am for on-the-hour sessions, 7:15am for quarter-hour, and 7:30am for half-hour. North Saanich Middle School requires reservations. Open Court play may be limited to 30 minutes when others are waiting. Under-12s need an adult. Eye protection is required for juniors and recommended for adults. Loaner gear is free — [Panorama Drop-in Sports](https://www.panoramarecreation.ca/drop-activities/sports)
- Panorama offers pickleball by reservation seasonally, plus year-round open court play (basketball, pickleball, volleyball, badminton). Recurring weekly or biweekly slots can be requested via "Book a Court" — [Panorama Fall 2025 guide (snippet)](https://issuu.com/panoramarec/docs/fall_2025_program_guide); [Panorama 2026 Spring/Summer guide](https://issuu.com/panoramarec/docs/2026_spring_summer_program_guide)
- Panorama phone numbers: 250-656-7271 (from the website), with 250-656-7055 cited elsewhere — [Panorama](https://www.panoramarecreation.ca/drop-activities/sports)
- Single sessions can be booked up to 7 days ahead online or at reception — [Panorama Spring 2023 brochure (dated)](https://issuu.com/panoramarec/docs/spring_2023_brochure_final/s/19555937)

### Inferences
- Bookings come up on a rolling one-week window opening Friday mornings, which suggests popular slots fill quickly.

### Gaps
- No current (fall/winter 2026) day and time grid was retrievable. The activity-schedules page didn't list pickleball times, and the seasonal schedules are Issuu flipbooks I couldn't parse. Drop-in admission fees for pickleball were not found.

## New courts opened or planned 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Two openings: the Centennial Park Multi-Sport Box (4 covered, lit courts, opened Sept 2, 2025), and the Centennial Court #1 conversion to 4 dedicated courts (approved June 2026, built Aug–Sept 2026). One closure: Wain Road Park, North Saanich, May 2024. I found no confirmed new courts in Sidney or North Saanich.

### Cited Findings
- Multi-Sport Box opened Sept 2, 2025 — [Central Saanich](https://www.centralsaanich.ca/node/3617); [CRD project page](https://www.crd.ca/projects/current-projects-initiatives/centennial-park-multi-sport-box)
- Court #1 relined into 4 dedicated courts, construction Aug–Sept 2026, total capacity 8 courts — [Central Saanich, June 25, 2026](https://www.centralsaanich.ca/node/3989)
- Wain Road Park closed May 7, 2024, and the judicial review was dropped in Feb 2025 — [Capital Daily](https://www.capitaldaily.ca/news/judicial-review-request-rescinded-north-saanich-pickleball-saga-plays-on)
- Blue Heron Park in North Saanich was discussed as a replacement site at an estimated $674K. Search snippets cite this cost but I couldn't verify a primary source. SPPA had pursued a court hub there with Peninsula Soccer — [Oak Bay News, undated](https://www.oakbaynews.com/sports/north-saanichs-blue-heron-park-could-host-pickleball-courts-529314)
- In 2024, residents presented a 400-signature petition opposing outdoor pickleball at Cy Hampson Park in North Saanich, citing noise (search snippet) — [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/10683796/pickleball-controversy-north-saanich/amp)
- Earlier Sidney proposals: Tulista Park (2 courts, about 2021) and Rotary Park (in North Saanich, near the airport; the Victoria Airport Authority rejected it). Neither was built as far as I could find — [Peninsula News Review (syndicated)](https://bpbpd.wpengine.com/news/sidney-hits-ball-back-into-the-court-of-pickleball-association); [Oak Bay News](https://www.oakbaynews.com/news/peninsula-pickleball-players-praises-sidneys-investigation-of-courts-at-north-saanichs-rotary-park/)

### Inferences
- Noise has shaped where courts get built on the peninsula. Central Saanich's success came from putting courts in an existing sports park (Centennial) with a roofed box. North Saanich residential-adjacent sites (Wain Road, Cy Hampson) have been blocked.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025–2026 source confirming any Blue Heron Park, Sidney (Tulista), or North Saanich reopening decision. The North Saanich site blocked fetches, so a late-2026 council update could have been missed.
- A completion or grand-opening notice for Centennial Court #1 was not found. Completion is inferred from the bookings page.
