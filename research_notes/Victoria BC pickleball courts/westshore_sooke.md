# Pickleball Courts: West Shore (Langford, Colwood, Metchosin, Highlands) and Sooke — Inventory as of Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-24. About 25 tool calls. Some official pages (wspr.ca PDFs, CRD SEAPARC schedule, Pickleheads, a Metchosin press-release PDF) could not be fetched or returned empty, so some details are missing. Source dates are given per venue.

## Which parks/facilities have pickleball? (location-by-location inventory)

### Takeaway
The West Shore + Sooke has about 7 confirmed pickleball venues:
- **West Shore Parks & Recreation (WSPR) / Juan de Fuca Rec Centre, 1767 Island Hwy, Colwood:** 6 outdoor pickleball courts opened in June 2025 (the strongest public outdoor hub), plus tennis courts shared with pickleball and indoor drop-in.
- **Victoria Pickleball Centre, Westhills, Langford:** 6 private indoor dedicated courts, opened April 2024.
- **Rally at Royal Bay, Colwood:** 4 new outdoor courts, opened June 2026.
- **Ernhill Park, Langford:** 1 outdoor court.
- **YMCA-YWCA Westhills, Langford:** indoor gym, booked by phone.
- **Throup Road courts, Sooke:** 3 outdoor courts, home of the Sooke Pickleball Club.
- **SEAPARC, Sooke:** indoor courts; drop-in runs at Journey Middle School.

I found no pickleball venues in Metchosin or Highlands. Many parks named in the brief (Westhills park, City Centre Park, Ruth King, Glen Lake, Herm Williams, Royal Roads) showed no evidence of pickleball courts.

### Cited Findings

**1. West Shore Parks & Recreation (WSPR) outdoor pickleball courts — 1767 Island Hwy (Hwy 14), Colwood (Juan de Fuca Recreation Centre grounds)**
- **Opening:** Officially opened June 13, 2025 with a ribbon-cutting. Construction ran February–May 2025. — [Island Social Trends, June 2025](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/); [Metchosin news, June 2025](https://www.metchosin.ca/node/905)
- **Location on site:** Across from the golf fieldhouse, next to the skatepark and tennis courts. The courts replaced the beach volleyball courts next to the tennis courts. — [Island Social Trends](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/); [CHEK News](https://cheknews.ca/really-excited-6-new-pickleball-courts-coming-to-west-shore-1220807/)
- **Court count (sources conflict):**
  - Island Social Trends, the Metchosin page and the WSPR announcement say "four dedicated pickleball courts", alongside courts still shared with tennis. — [Metchosin](https://www.metchosin.ca/node/905); [Island Social Trends](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/)
  - CHEK (planning stage), VRPA (Victoria Regional Pickleball Association) and a Roots in Victoria blog say **6** outdoor dedicated courts. — [CHEK News](https://cheknews.ca/really-excited-6-new-pickleball-courts-coming-to-west-shore-1220807/); [VRPA Where to Play, 2026](https://secure.pickleballcanada.org/club/victoria-regional-pickleball-association/1033/where-to-play/7652/); [Roots in Victoria, Aug 31 2026](https://www.rootsinvictoria.com/blog/best-pickleball-courts-greater-victoria/)
  - The booking breakdown numbers courts 1–6. — [Island Social Trends](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/)
- **Surface:** A 2-inch asphalt base with 5 coats of plexi, built by Tomko Sports. — [Island Social Trends](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/)
- **Fencing:** Low fencing for spectators. — same source
- **Cost:** Budget $400,000; final cost $468,000. — same source
- **Booking and fees:**
  - Courts 1–4 can be reserved online, same day or up to 1 week ahead, at **$16 per 2-hour session**.
  - Courts 5–6 are drop-in, first come, first served.
  - — [Island Social Trends](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/)
- **On-site rules sign:**
  - Courts are open 7:00 AM–10:00 PM unless posted.
  - WSPR programs have priority.
  - Drop-in play is in 30-minute blocks; reserved play is in 120-minute blocks, booked via a QR code.
  - Contact: 250-478-8384, feedback@wspr.ca.
  - The sign is undated. — [WSPR pickleball rules sign PDF](https://explore.wspr.ca/WSPR-Sign-Pickleball%20Rules-revB.pdf)
  - An earlier article says the courts open "morning to dusk" in summer. — [Island Social Trends](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/)
- **Lights:** No source mentions lights on the pickleball courts.
- **Who uses it:** Residents of Langford, Colwood, Metchosin, View Royal and Highlands (the five owner municipalities). — [Island Social Trends](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/); [Metchosin](https://www.metchosin.ca/node/905)
- **Design input:** The Greater Victoria Pickleball Association gave feedback on the design. — [Island Social Trends](https://islandsocialtrends.ca/new-pickleball-courts-officially-open-at-westshore-parks-and-rec/)
- **Note:** CHEK and VRPA call the site "near the Q Centre". Both the Q Centre and the Juan de Fuca Rec Centre are at 1767 Island Hwy. Some directories place it in Colwood, others in View Royal/Langford. Metchosin News places WSPR in Colwood.

### Inferences
- **Public outdoor dedicated courts total about 14:** WSPR 6 (or 4 dedicated + 2), Royal Bay 4, Throup Rd 3, Ernhill 1. Indoor dedicated: Victoria Pickleball Centre 6 (private). Indoor multi-use: the WSPR Indoor Sports Complex, SEAPARC/Journey Middle School and the Y.
- **Six vs four at WSPR:** Most likely 6 new courts were built, with 4 reservable and 2 drop-in. The "four dedicated" wording in the press release may refer to the reservable courts. This fits the courts 1–6 booking breakdown.
- **Parks named in the brief:** Westhills (outdoor), City Centre Park, Ruth King, Glen Lake, Herm Williams and Royal Roads showed no evidence of pickleball courts in any source. Langford's own court list names only Centennial Park (tennis) plus basketball courts. "Colwood Pickleball complex" most likely refers to the WSPR courts.
- **Rough coordinates (my estimates, not from a source; verify before use):**
  - WSPR/JdF Rec, 1767 Island Hwy: ~48.446, -123.478
  - Victoria Pickleball Centre, West Shore Pkwy: ~48.44, -123.53
  - Royal Bay, Producers Way: ~48.40, -123.47
  - Throup Rd, Sooke: ~48.38, -123.72
  - SEAPARC, 2168 Phillips Rd: ~48.39, -123.72

## Indoor drop-in schedules (WSPR, SEAPARC)

### Takeaway
WSPR runs indoor pickleball (reservable court times plus drop-in) at the Indoor Sports Complex, 1767 Island Hwy. Schedules are published seasonally as PDFs. SEAPARC runs drop-in pickleball at Journey Middle School, September to June. I could not read either current schedule.

### Cited Findings
- **WSPR Fall 2025 sheet:** Indoor Sports Complex, Sept 2–Dec 20, 2025, with weekday pickleball sessions around midday and in the evening. The schedule changes on holidays, pro-d days and special events. Phone 250-478-8384. — [WSPR PDF](https://explore.wspr.ca/DROPIN_SPORTS_Sept-Dec2025.pdf)
- **WSPR Spring 2026 sheet:** Apr 18–Jun 27, 2026 (last updated May 1, 2026). — [WSPR PDF](https://explore.wspr.ca/DROPIN_SPORTS_Spring2026_Apr18-June27_2.pdf)
- **WSPR earlier sheet:** Fall 2024 (Sep 3–Dec 21). — [WSPR PDF](https://explore.wspr.ca/DROPIN_SPORTS_Fall2024_Sep3-Dec21.pdf)
- **SEAPARC drop-in:** At Journey Middle School, September to June, all ages and abilities, limited equipment. The CRD page shows only a rolling 2-week window. — [CRD SEAPARC sports schedule](https://www.crd.ca/seaparc/drop-in-schedules/sports-schedule)
- **Indoor play in Langford:** The Victoria Pickleball Centre is open daily 6:00 AM–9:30 PM (2024) with drop-in, leagues and lessons. — [Langford](https://langford.ca/people-are-so-excited-victoria-pickleball-centre-now-open-in-langford/); [Roots in Victoria](https://www.rootsinvictoria.com/blog/best-pickleball-courts-greater-victoria/)

## New courts opened or planned 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Openings in this period:
- **Victoria Pickleball Centre** (indoor, Langford): April 2024.
- **Centennial Park tennis rebuild** (Langford): April 2025; not pickleball-specific.
- **WSPR outdoor courts** (Colwood): June 13, 2025.
