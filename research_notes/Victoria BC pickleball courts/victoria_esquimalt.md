# Pickleball courts in the City of Victoria and the Township of Esquimalt (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Scope: City of Victoria and Township of Esquimalt only. Three sources were cited most: the City of Victoria Outdoor Recreation Facilities page (current court table, fetched Nov 2026), the City news release on Topaz Park (8 May 2026) and Esquimalt's pickleball pages (2026 season).

**Coordinates.** No source gave latitude or longitude. The figures in the table are approximate positions I estimated from the street addresses. Geocode them before you put them on a map.

## Which parks/facilities in City of Victoria and Esquimalt have pickleball courts or lines as of 2026?

### Takeaway
The City of Victoria has 21 public outdoor pickleball courts across 6 parks:
- **Topaz Park:** 11 dedicated, lit courts. This is the largest hub on the Island and opened in May 2026.
- **Beacon Hill Park:** 3 dedicated courts.
- **Franklin Green:** 1 dedicated court.
- **Central Park, Oaklands Park and Barnard Park:** 2 courts each, shared with tennis.

Esquimalt has no Township-run outdoor park courts. Its main venue is 4 seasonal indoor courts in the Archie Browning curling arena (late May to September). In winter, drop-in moves to the Esquimalt Rec Centre. Esquimalt High School also has 2 outdoor tennis courts with pickleball lines.

### Cited Findings

**City of Victoria: court table, booking and fees**
- The City of Victoria court table lists:
  - Barnard Park, 300 Barnard Ave: 2 mixed-use tennis/pickleball courts.
  - Beacon Hill Park, 100 Cook St: 3 tennis and 3 pickleball courts.
  - Central Park, 2275 Quadra St: 2 mixed-use courts.
  - Franklin Green, 1045 Mason St: 1 pickleball court.
  - Oaklands Park, 1550 Kings Rd: 2 mixed-use courts and 2 tennis courts.
  - Topaz Park, 841 Finlayson St: 11 pickleball courts.
  - Tennis-only parks: Banfield (521 Craigflower Rd), Hollywood (1700 Fairfield Rd), Stadacona (1490 Pandora Ave) and Todd Park (100 & 111 Montreal St).
  - [City of Victoria – Outdoor Recreation Facilities](https://www.victoria.ca/parks-recreation/recreation/outdoor-recreation-facilities)
- Booking rules for City courts:
  - Courts can be reserved up to 3 weeks ahead, for up to 3 hours.
  - Fees are $10/hour for adults and $5/hour for youth.
  - Hours are 7 a.m. to dusk, and all bookings are final.
  - Drop-in players must leave when a booking starts.
  - There are "dedicated drop-in-only courts at Beacon Hill Park."
  - [City of Victoria – Outdoor Recreation Facilities](https://www.victoria.ca/parks-recreation/recreation/outdoor-recreation-facilities)
- The City launched its online tennis/pickleball court booking system on 8 April 2026. Some locations keep dedicated drop-in courts. — [City of Victoria – Victoria Serves Up New Court Booking System](https://www.victoria.ca/node/3236)

**Topaz Park**
- The Topaz Park hub opened in May 2026 (release dated 8 May 2026), as part of phase two of the Topaz Park Improvement Plan.
  - It has 11 dedicated courts with permanent nets, fencing, a shade structure and lighting for evening play.
  - The City says it "offers 10 other pickleball courts at Beacon Hill Park, Central Park, Oaklands Park and Barnard Park."
  - [City of Victoria – Island's Largest Pickleball Hub Opens at Topaz Park](https://www.victoria.ca/node/3282)
- A CTV News article also covers the 11 new Topaz courts. — [CTV News – Victoria opens 11 new pickleball courts at Topaz Park](https://guelph.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/vancouver-island/article/victoria-opens-11-new-pickleball-courts-at-topaz-park/)

**Esquimalt**
- Esquimalt High School has 2 outdoor hard courts with lines over a tennis court, so the net is slightly taller than a regulation pickleball net. — [Pickleheads – Esquimalt High School](https://www.pickleheads.com/courts/ca/british-columbia/esquimalt/esquimalt-high-school)
- Esquimalt Recreation Centre: third-party directories list 2 indoor courts, pay to play. — [Pickleheads – Esquimalt Recreation Centre](https://www.pickleheads.com/courts/ca/british-columbia/esquimalt/esquimalt-recreation-centre)
- 2026 court rentals at Archie Browning:
  - Rentals started 23 May 2026, with 4 courts, pickleball only.
  - $18.00 + GST per court for each 1-hour booking.
  - Up to 8 players per court booking; one person may book up to 2 courts.
  - Book up to 7 days ahead, online, in person at 527 Fraser St or by phone at 250-412-8500.
  - No refunds with less than 3 days' notice. Players bring their own equipment.
  - [Esquimalt – Pickleball court rentals and programs](https://www.esquimalt.ca/node/8492)
- Season and FAQ details:
  - The 2026 summer program and rentals run in the Archie Browning Sports Centre curling arena from 24 May to 5 September 2026 (drop-in from 23 May) and relaunch in spring 2027.
  - In winter the curling ice returns, and drop-in and lessons move to the Esquimalt Recreation Centre.
  - Registration is required for drop-ins, and a facilitator runs drop-in sessions.
  - Some programs require a DUPR rating.
  - [Esquimalt – Pickleball](https://www.esquimalt.ca/node/8490); [Esquimalt – Pickleball FAQ](https://www.esquimalt.ca/node/8491)
- Esquimalt first offered seasonal pickleball at Archie Browning on 26 May 2025. — [Victoria News – Esquimalt unveils summer pickleball initiatives](https://vicnews.com/marketplace/grab-your-paddle-esquimalt-unveils-exciting-summer-pickleball-initiatives-7350937); [Victoria News – Esquimalt launches seasonal indoor pickleball courts](https://vicnews.com/marketplace/esquimalt-launches-seasonal-indoor-pickleball-courts-for-all-ages-7381485)

### Inferences
- **Court count:** 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 10 "other" courts, which matches the City's "10 other courts." The City's sentence leaves out Franklin Green, but its court table includes it. City of Victoria total: about 21 outdoor courts, 15 of them dedicated (Topaz 11, Beacon Hill 3, Franklin Green 1) and 6 shared with tennis.
- **Beacon Hill status:** The City now lists Beacon Hill's 3 courts as pickleball courts, but VRPA still calls them "temporary." They were likely never rebuilt as purpose-built courts.
- **Esquimalt outdoors:** Esquimalt has no Township-run outdoor park courts. The only outdoor option is the school court at Esquimalt High School.
- **Hampton Park:** Out of scope (Saanich). Other researchers covering Saanich should include it.

## Which rec centres run indoor pickleball drop-ins?

### Takeaway
I could confirm indoor public drop-in at only two places:
- **Esquimalt:** Esquimalt Recreation Centre in fall and winter, and Archie Browning in summer. Registration is required for drop-ins.
- **James Bay Community School Centre:** Tuesday and Thursday gym drop-in. The listing is undated.

The City of Victoria runs no indoor pickleball of its own:
- Crystal Pool closed permanently on 25 September 2026.
- The interim Victoria Recreation Centre at 851 Broughton St, with a gym, opens in early 2027.
- No source mentions pickleball in either facility.

I found no evidence of drop-in at Oaklands, Fernwood, Vic West, Burnside Gorge or Quadra Village community centres.

### Cited Findings
- **James Bay Community School Centre (140 Oswego St):**
  - Gym drop-in for adults 16+, equipment provided; pay and sign in at the desk.
  - Tuesdays 1–3 pm on 1 court, $2.50–$3.00. Thursdays 7–9 pm on 3 courts, $3.50.
  - The listing is undated, so it may be stale.
  - [PlayPickleball – James Bay Community Center](https://www.playpickleball.com/where-to-play-pickleball-near-me-map/places/canada-british-columbia-victoria-james-bay-community-center/); [Active.com – Drop-in Pickleball (16+), 2024](https://www.active.com/victoria-bc/classes/drop-in-pickleball-16-2024)
- **Esquimalt:**
  - Winter drop-in runs at the Esquimalt Recreation Centre, 527 Fraser St; summer drop-in is at the Archie Browning courts.
  - Registration is required, and a facilitator oversees sessions.
  - The Rec Centre is open 5:30 am to 10:30 pm.
  - [Esquimalt – Pickleball FAQ](https://www.esquimalt.ca/node/8491)
- **Crystal Pool and the interim Victoria Recreation Centre:**
  - Crystal Pool and Fitness Centre closed permanently on 25 September 2026.
  - Programs move to the interim Victoria Recreation Centre, 851 Broughton St, opening early 2027. It will have a gymnasium, squash courts and multipurpose rooms.
  - Pickleball is not mentioned.
  - [City of Victoria – Crystal Pool Closure Marks Next Phase](https://www.victoria.ca/node/3559)
- **Permanent replacement:** The replacement recreation centre ($209.2M) is projected to open by late 2030, and the project includes a gymnasium. — [City of Victoria – Crystal Pool Replacement Project](https://www.victoria.ca/city-government/projects/crystal-pool-replacement-project); [CTV News – Last week of operation for Crystal Pool](https://www.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/vancouver-island/article/last-week-of-operation-for-victorias-55-year-old-crystal-pool/)

  - The Victoria Conference Centre has offered seasonal winter drop-in in Carson Hall. It is temporary and I could not confirm it for 2026–27. — [Tourism Victoria – Pickleball at the Victoria Conference Centre](https://www.tourismvictoria.com/intention/movement-and-mindfulness/pickleball-at-the-victoria-conference-centre)
- **Out of scope:** The Victoria Pickleball Centre (dedicated, private, indoor, 6 courts) is at 2950 West Shore Pkwy, which is Langford/West Shore, not the City of Victoria. — [Victoria Pickleball Centre (Playbypoint)](https://victoriapickleballcentre.playbypoint.com/)

## Any new courts opened or planned (2024–2026), or courts removed/restricted due to noise complaints?

### Takeaway
- **New:** Topaz Park's 11 dedicated, lit courts opened in May 2026. This is the big addition, and it was planned partly as the noise-safe replacement for removed courts.
- **Esquimalt:** Added seasonal indoor courts at Archie Browning in 2025, repeated in 2026 and planned for 2027.
- **Victoria booking:** Online court booking with fees started in April 2026.
- **Removed:** Pickleball lines were taken off the Todd Park tennis courts (James Bay) on 31 March 2022 after neighbour complaints. Players were moved to Central Park.
- **Esquimalt plans:** I found no Esquimalt plan for outdoor courts.

### Cited Findings
- **Todd Park removal (2022):**
  - The City removed pickleball lines from the Todd Park tennis courts on 31 March 2022 after neighbour noise complaints. The courts are about 17 m from the nearest home.
  - Players moved to Central Park, with Oaklands, Barnard Park and Franklin Green also listed as alternatives.
  - [Global News – Victoria pickleball group ousted over noise complaints](https://globalnews.ca/news/8702514/victoria-pickleball-group-ousted-noise-complaints); [CTV – Popular Victoria pickleball court closing due to noise complaints](https://vancouverisland.ctvnews.ca/popular-victoria-pickleball-court-closing-due-to-noise-complaints-1.5828204)
- Seniors protested the closures by playing outside City Hall in 2022. — [CTV – 'Long live pickleball' protest](https://vancouverisland.ctvnews.ca/long-live-pickleball-seniors-play-pickleball-outside-victoria-city-hall-to-protest-court-closures-1.5854209); [Global News – Victoria pickleball protest](https://globalnews.ca/news/8743501/victoria-pickleball-protest)
- **Beacon Hill pilot (2022):**
  - Council approved a pilot of 2, possibly 3, courts in a former parking area of Beacon Hill Park.
  - Accessibility advocates raised concerns about the court upgrades there. That CTV article now returns 404, so it was verified only from the search snippet.
  - [CTV – Pickleball court pilot project coming to Beacon Hill Park](https://vancouverisland.ctvnews.ca/pickleball-court-pilot-project-coming-to-beacon-hill-park-1.5879600)
- **Topaz as the replacement site:**
  - The City said dedicated Topaz courts would be placed where noise would not affect residents.
  - BCRPA guidance cited in coverage recommends courts be at least 50 m from homes.
  - [CTV – Pickleball's growth raises a racket in Victoria](https://vancouverisland.ctvnews.ca/pickleball-s-growth-raises-a-racket-in-victoria-amid-bans-over-noise-complaints-1.5883854); [City of Victoria – Topaz Park Improvements](https://www.victoria.ca/city-government/projects/topaz-park-improvements)
- The Topaz hub opened in May 2026 with 11 courts, lights and a shade structure. A fenced dog park in the same phase is due later in 2026. — [City of Victoria – Topaz hub opens](https://www.victoria.ca/node/3282)
- The online booking system launched 8 April 2026. — [City of Victoria](https://www.victoria.ca/node/3236)
- Esquimalt ran seasonal indoor courts at Archie Browning from 26 May 2025, again from 23–24 May to 5 September 2026, with a relaunch planned for spring 2027. — [Esquimalt – Pickleball](https://www.esquimalt.ca/node/8490); [Victoria News](https://vicnews.com/marketplace/esquimalt-launches-seasonal-indoor-pickleball-courts-for-all-ages-7381485)
- A 2024 regional Pickleball Strategy covered Victoria, Saanich, Oak Bay and Esquimalt. I did not find an Esquimalt outdoor build item in the excerpts I reviewed. — [Saanich-hosted Pickleball Strategy, Feb 2024](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Parks~Recreation~and~Community~Services/Documents/Planning/Feb%2027-24%20FINAL%20PickleballStrategy_20240226.pdf)

### Inferences
- Todd Park is now tennis-only: the City table lists 3 tennis courts and no pickleball there, so the 2022 removal still stands.
- With Topaz open, the City may later reduce lines on the shared tennis courts. No source confirms this, so it is speculation.

- I found no Esquimalt council report on outdoor courts. The Council Priorities Plan update (January 2026) excerpts did not mention pickleball.
- Topaz court surface and hours (whether the lights extend play past "dusk") are not stated.
