# Pickleball courts in the District of Oak Bay and the Town of View Royal (as of Nov 2026)

Researched 2026-11-24. The source date is given for each finding. Coordinates and addresses marked "(unverified)" come from general knowledge, not from a fetched source. Check them before publishing.

## Venue inventory summary (quick reference)

### Takeaway
Oak Bay has **one outdoor pickleball venue**: Carnarvon Park, with 5 dedicated courts in the former lacrosse box. It has **one indoor venue**: Henderson Recreation Centre, with bookable court rentals plus reserved social drop-in. **View Royal has no known public pickleball courts.** The only tennis courts it lists are at Helmcken Centennial Park, and no source confirms pickleball lines there. View Royal residents are served by the 6 dedicated outdoor courts at West Shore Parks & Recreation (WSPR) in **Colwood**, which opened June 2025 and are outside View Royal.

## Oak Bay noise disputes and outcomes

### Takeaway
Carnarvon has been Greater Victoria's flagship pickleball noise dispute since about 2014. Oak Bay's response came in steps. In 2021 it budgeted up to $70K to move the 5 courts into the lacrosse box. In 2022 it added about $25K of acoustic fencing, with $4K from VRPA. The 2024 regional strategy still flags Carnarvon as below the recommended setback (33 m existing vs 45 m with barriers, or 25 m with barriers plus quieter equipment). The long-term fix is now part of the Carnarvon Park master-plan redevelopment, which includes new acoustic-panelled courts.

### Cited Findings
- Pickleball started at Carnarvon in 2014 on converted tennis courts, and noise has been an issue ever since. The original courts sat about 10 ft from neighbours' fences on Allenby St — [Victoria News, 2022-06-24](https://vicnews.com/2022/06/24/oak-bay-battles-pickleball-noise-with-rubber-fencing/); [Victoria News search snippet, 2021](https://www.vicnews.com/news/closed-courts-not-an-option-for-oak-bay-despite-pickleball-noise-84389).
- 2021: council ruled out closing the courts ("Closed courts not an option") and approved up to $70,000 to move them into the Carnarvon lacrosse box — [Victoria News, 2021](https://www.vicnews.com/news/oak-bay-budgets-70000-to-move-pickleball-courts-to-lacrosse-box-85462); [Committee of the Whole minutes, 19 Apr 2021](https://oakbay.civicweb.net/document/72417/).
- June 2022 (Committee of the Whole, June 20): complaints had dropped but continued, and residents said this reflected exhaustion rather than a fix. The move took courts away from Allenby St homes but brought them closer to homes at Townley and Henderson. Council approved rubberized acoustic fencing (AcoustiFence/Acoustiblok) adding 6 ft above the box's existing 5 ft plywood base, at about $25,000, and accepted VRPA's $4,000 contribution. Coun. Cairine Green described a three-part plan: short-term fix, long-term solution and ongoing research — [Victoria News, 2022-06-24](https://vicnews.com/2022/06/24/oak-bay-battles-pickleball-noise-with-rubber-fencing/); [CotW minutes, 20 Jun 2022](https://oakbay.civicweb.net/document/84280/).
- Carnarvon's sound measurements were later used as a test case in North Saanich's Wain Park noise dispute — [Sooke News Mirror search snippet](https://www.sookenewsmirror.com/local-news/north-saanich-pickleballers-head-to-oak-bay-for-noise-tests-at-carnarvon-7359681). Noise testing at the Oak Bay courts was also reported — [CFAX](https://www.cfax1070.com/news/noise-level-testing-at-oak-bay-pickleball-courts-wednesday.html).
- In the 2024 regional strategy, Carnarvon has a 33 m setback, has neighbourhood sound concerns and "limited barriers". It can meet the target "with barriers and quieter equipment": 45 m needed with a barrier, 25 m with barrier plus quieter equipment. The strategy says Carnarvon "may require more involved effort, such as adjusting court layouts, moving one or all courts, or converting the courts to covered facilities" — [Strategy, Feb 2024](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Parks~Recreation~and~Community~Services/Documents/Planning/Feb%2027-24%20FINAL%20PickleballStrategy_20240226.pdf).
- The strategy rates Oak Bay at 1 court per 3,598 residents, "on the lower end of supply". It lists Henderson among tennis sites for a utilization review (Rec #7, tennis courts more than 50 m from homes that could become dedicated pickleball) — [Strategy](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Parks~Recreation~and~Community~Services/Documents/Planning/Feb%2027-24%20FINAL%20PickleballStrategy_20240226.pdf).

### Inferences
- I found no news since 2022 of new court closures or fresh complaints. The dispute appears to have moved into the park redevelopment process.

### Gaps
- No 2024 to 2026 council minutes on Carnarvon noise complaints were found.

## New or planned courts, 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Oak Bay's **Carnarvon Park redevelopment** will rebuild the racquet area as 5 pickleball courts plus 3 tennis courts with acoustic panels. Design packages were due summer 2026 and the work is phased over about 6 years, so court construction dates are unconfirmed as of Nov 2026. A 2024 idea for indoor pickleball under rental housing was scrapped. Nothing is planned within View Royal. The nearby WSPR courts in Colwood opened June 2025.

### Cited Findings
- May 2024 option: a 3-storey building with 24 affordable units over a year-round indoor pickleball facility, five indoor pickleball courts, two tennis courts and a plaza, sited on Henderson where the current courts and lacrosse box are — [Capital Daily, 2024-05-06](https://capitaldaily.ca/news/housing-indoor-pickleball-court-under-consideration-carnarvon-park). Council later dropped the rental-housing option unanimously after backlash — [Times Colonist, "Rental-housing option scrapped for Carnarvon Park" (2024)](https://www.timescolonist.com/local-news/rental-housing-option-scrapped-for-carnarvon-park-9520161); [Times Colonist, backlash](https://www.timescolonist.com/local-news/idea-of-rental-housing-in-carnarvon-park-draws-backlash-9490345). (Outcome taken from the headline and search summary. The article wasn't fetched.)
- May 2025: council awarded Shape Architecture $1,041,057 for design and engagement, with 8 tender-ready packages due by summer 2026 and changes spread over 6 years. The plan includes new tennis and pickleball courts with acoustic panels. The courts are the one asset expected to be out of service for an extended period, and pickleball work would be scheduled for low-play months, so players may need other courts during construction — [Oak Bay News, 2025-05-02](https://oakbaynews.com/2025/05/02/oak-bay-council-moves-ahead-with-plans-for-major-upgrade-to-carnarvon-park/).
- Dec 2025: the plan includes a racquet court area with 5 pickleball and 3 tennis courts, a dog park and a new rec centre. A public info session was set for Jan 8 (2026) at the Sports View Lounge — [Oak Bay News, 2025-12-28](https://oakbaynews.com/2025/12/28/info-session-brings-upgrades-to-oak-bays-carnarvon-park-into-focus/).
- An Aug 2026 local guide still describes 5 dedicated, reservable courts at Carnarvon in operation — [Roots in Victoria, 2026-08-31](https://www.rootsinvictoria.com/blog/best-pickleball-courts-greater-victoria/).
- View Royal: the Oct 2024 WSPR courts project (6 courts) is the only pickleball item on viewroyal.ca. It sits on WSPR property in Colwood — [viewroyal.ca, 2024-10-25](https://www.viewroyal.ca/EN/meta/new/2024-latest-news/west-shore-parks-recreation-moves-forward-with-new-pickleball-courts-amid-growing-community-dem.html).
- View Royal wasn't a partner in the 2024 regional strategy, whose partners were Victoria, Saanich, Oak Bay and Esquimalt — [Strategy](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Parks~Recreation~and~Community~Services/Documents/Planning/Feb%2027-24%20FINAL%20PickleballStrategy_20240226.pdf).

### Inferences
- The new pickleball net count at Carnarvon stays at 5. The change is relocation and sound mitigation, not added capacity. A temporary Carnarvon closure for construction is likely sometime in 2026 to 2032.

### Gaps
- Construction start, final court siting, whether the courts will be covered, and the cost of the Carnarvon racquet area were not found as of Nov 2026.
- No View Royal council minutes on pickleball were found. View Royal may have discussed pickleball lines at Helmcken Centennial Park, but no source confirms it.
